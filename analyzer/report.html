05 / MATCHED CONDITIONS

Wind on level roads Vetar na ravnom, pri sličnoj brzini

Matching criteria / Uslovi uparivanja

Same 10 km/h bin; speed difference ≤2 km/h, window speed range ≤6 km/h. Grade ±0.5%, pair difference ≤0.25 pp; altitude residual ≤2.5 m. Road vicinity ≤300 m, direction ≤15°, different trips, no reused windows. Outside temperature within 3 °C, battery within 5 °C, SOC within 10 pp, same rain/snow class, head/tailwind magnitude within 5 km/h. Weather ≤1 h old and ≤25 km away. Result requires ≥10 pairs, ≥5 km per direction, ≥2 trips on each side.

Vetar se računa iz pravca odakle duva; pozitivan uzdužni vetar je čeoni. Porede se slične ravne deonice pri istoj brzini. GPS tačke nisu nezavisne vožnje. Klima, saobraćaj, gume i model vetra ostaju ograničenja — ovo nije dokaz uzročnosti.

Comparable conditions / Upareni slični uslovi

How to read these comparisons / Kako čitati poređenja

Across all imported dates. Flat, steady segments; same road vicinity and direction, mean speed within 2 km/h and the same 10 km/h band, similar SOC, battery temperature and longitudinal wind. Tires and HVAC must be known. Except for the factor being compared, tire set, HVAC state, weather class and outside temperature must match. Temperature contrast: below 5 °C versus 15–25 °C. Results require ≥10 pairs, ≥5 km and ≥2 trips per side. / Prikazuje se povezanost u sličnim uslovima, ne dokaz uzroka. Pritisak guma, teret i saobraćaj mogu ostati nepoznati. Za klimu je ovo razlika ukupne potrošnje vozila, ne direktno merenje energije kompresora.

Use the desktop Edit / Uredi conditions button for dated tire sets or known constant HVAC periods. Exported tire history is read automatically, only from its recorded change date onward. / U programu unesi datume promene guma; poslednji profil se ne primenjuje unazad.

Full equivalent cycles (FEC) / Ekvivalentni puni ciklusi

Partial cycles → FEC / Delimični ciklusi → FEC

Concept: FEC = partial cycles × depth of discharge / 100. A cycle includes the charge/discharge traversal of that window; count the discharge once. Two 25–75% cycles = one nominal FEC. Actual recorded FEC here uses discharge kWh, because displayed vehicle SOC and cell SOC are not the same scale. / Primer je nominalni ekvivalent; stvarni zapis se računa iz energije. kWh = energija, kW = snaga.

Shallower cycling can allow more partial cycles and sometimes more total throughput before the same capacity loss; the improvement depends on SOC window, temperature, rates and cell design. Equal FEC does not imply equal degradation. / Plići ciklusi mogu dati više delimičnih ciklusa, a nekad i veći ukupan protok do istog pada kapaciteta. Isti FEC nije ista degradacija; nema univerzalnog množitelja za ovaj CATL paket.

Recorded trip SOC windows / Zabeležene dubine pražnjenja u vožnjama

These are trip SOC falls, not complete counted battery cycles. No unstored driving/charging is reconstructed. / Ovo su padovi SOC-a po vožnji, ne broj celih ciklusa.

Recorded discharge kWh ÷ fixed initial usable kWh. Charge-session count, net energy and Ah-based EFC are different measures. Lifetime-counter deltas cover only observed counter intervals; a decrease or implausible jump disables the combined estimate. / Broj punjenja nije broj ekvivalentnih ciklusa. Osnova je stalni početni kapacitet, ne opadajući SOH.

Published aging tests / Objavljena ispitivanja starenja

These studies do not predict how many cycles your CATL pack has left. Calendar age, temperature, SOC window, charge/discharge rates and cell design matter. The 80% capacity level is a comparison convention, not an instruction to replace or reset a battery. / Preostali vek se ne izračunava iz jedne univerzalne brojke ciklusa.

Method & files / Metod i fajlovi

Positive power = discharge. Integration uses intervals ≤30 s, never missing values filled with zero. Whole-trip consumption/capacity requires ≥95% coverage; effective capacity additionally requires a SOC fall ≥10 pp. It is not gross capacity or a laboratory health test. Exports are deduplicated by vehicle/timestamp. Original ZIPs are unchanged. Times are UTC.

Izveštaj ne menja BMS. Nepoznat SOH pojedinačne ćelije se ne izmišlja. Izveštaj sadrži lične putanje — deli ga svesno.
