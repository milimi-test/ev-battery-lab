Drag to pan; +/− to zoom. Cursor values keep original units. Overlay scales each signal to its visible min–max range, not battery percentage. Gaps over 30 s are disconnected. / Povuci za pomeranje; preklop koristi relativnu skalu svakog signala.

Trips & capacity / Vožnje i kapacitet

Energy is integrated from power only across gaps ≤30 s. Consumption and capacity require ≥95% time coverage. Capacity needs a SOC fall ≥10 percentage points and represents effective energy per displayed SOC span. / Procena nije bruto kapacitet niti laboratorijski SOH.

Modelled wind / Vetar iz prognoze

These comparisons describe recorded conditions; they do not isolate causal effects. Speed, acceleration, temperature, slope and wind can change together. Grade uses ≥200 m GPS segments and altitude, which can be noisy. Wind is a ≤1 h old model sample within 50 km; positive headwind opposes travel. / Poređenje nije dokaz zasebnog uticaja vetra ili nagiba. Ispod 5 km u grupi označeno je malo podataka.

Route sketch / Skica putanje

No online map calls. Schematic trace, not road navigation. Segments separated by time gaps are not joined. / Lokalna skica, bez onlajn mape.

Elevation / Nadmorska visina

Recorded GPS altitude along the trace; cumulative distance includes only connected segments. / Snimljena GPS visina, ne model terena.

Cell groups / Ćelijske grupe

Fresh unique array reads only. Index = BMS group, not necessarily a single physical cell. Voltage deviation is measured against the pack mean at the same scan. Lowest count includes ties. / SOH grupe je samo direktno očitan BMS podatak; ne izvodi se iz napona.

Method, coverage & files / Metod, pokrivenost i fajlovi

Positive battery power = discharge, negative = energy entering the battery. Regeneration and grid charging are not interchangeable. Speed statistics include only intervals ≥3 km/h with fresh speed and complete energy. Temperature and speed bins therefore exclude idling. Session consumption includes recorded stops. Missing power is never filled with zero. Repeated exports are deduplicated. Times in tables/charts are UTC. Input archives are not extracted or uploaded. / Pozitivna snaga je pražnjenje, negativna ulaz energije. Nepoznati podaci se ne dopunjuju nulom. Izveštaj sadrži privatnu putanju — deli ga svesno.
